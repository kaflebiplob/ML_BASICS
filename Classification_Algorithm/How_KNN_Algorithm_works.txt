## KNN (K-Nearest Neighbors)

1. **Define the K value.**
2. **Calculate the distance** from the new point to all other points using **Euclidean Distance**.

$$
d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}
$$

3. **Sort all the distances** from smallest to largest.
4. **Select the K nearest neighbors.**
5. **Majority Count** → The class with the most neighbors is the prediction.

### Other than Classification

For regression, we take the **mean (average) of the K nearest neighbors**.

### Dimensions

KNN can work with **N dimensions (features)**.

### Hyperparameter

**K → Number of nearest neighbors**

K is often chosen as an **odd number** in binary classification to avoid ties.
