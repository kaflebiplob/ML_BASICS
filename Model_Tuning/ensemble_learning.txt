## Ensemble Learning

**Ensemble Learning** is a machine learning technique where we combine the predictions of **multiple models** to make a better and more reliable prediction.

The basic idea is:

> **“Listen to the crowd.”**

Instead of relying on a single model, we use multiple models and combine their results.

### Machine Learning

A basic machine learning process is:

```text
Training Data → Train the Model → Test the Model → Prediction
```

In **Ensemble Learning**, multiple models work together:

```text
Data
 ↓
Multiple Models
 ↓
Combine Their Predictions
 ↓
Final Prediction
```

### Types of Ensemble Learning

1. **Bagging**

   * Trains multiple models independently using different samples of the training data.
   * Their predictions are then combined to make the final prediction.

2. **Boosting**

   * Trains models sequentially, where each new model focuses more on the mistakes made by previous models.
   * The models are combined to produce a stronger model.

3. **Stacking**

   * Combines predictions from multiple different models.
   * Another model, called a **meta-model**, learns how to combine those predictions to make the final prediction.
