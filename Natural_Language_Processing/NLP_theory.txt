# NLP

**NLP (Natural Language Processing)** is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that helps computers understand, interpret, and generate human language.

## Why do we need NLP?

Computers understand information in the form of numbers (ultimately represented as 0s and 1s), so NLP is needed to help computers process and understand human languages.

**Real-world examples:** ChatGPT, Alexa, Siri

**Other examples:** Spam Detection in Gmail, Sentiment Analysis, etc.

## Approaches of NLP

### 1. Rule-Based Approach (Old-School NLP)

Uses predefined rules to process and understand text.

**Limitations:**

* Can't handle complex, ambiguous, or new patterns.
* Not scalable for large amounts of data.

### 2. Statistical (Machine Learning) Approach

Language is treated as data, and machine learning algorithms learn patterns from it.

**Models used:** Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, SVM, etc.

Text needs to be converted into numbers using techniques such as:

* Bag of Words (BoW)
* TF-IDF

**Limitations:**

* Can lose the context and meaning of words.
* Struggles with things like sarcasm and the sequence of words.

### 3. Deep Learning-Based Approach (Modern NLP)

Uses neural networks to automatically learn complex patterns and context from text.

Models can better understand meaning, context, word order, and more.

**Popular architectures:**

* RNN (Recurrent Neural Networks)
* LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)
* GRU (Gated Recurrent Units)
* Transformers (BERT, GPT, T5, etc.)
